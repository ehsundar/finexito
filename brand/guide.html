Brand guidelines · v1

Money that opens a way out.

The idea

Finexito is the financial exit: the point where your money works hard enough that you get to choose. The mark is a door left open. It isn't a vault or a chart or a coin. It's a way through, already ajar. Everything else in the brand stays quiet so the door can say it.

Logo

The full logo for headers and documents, the mark on its own where space is tight, and the app icon wherever the platform puts it in a tile.

Do

  • Leave clear space around the logo equal to the door's width.
  • Use the mark down to 24 px, and the favicon below that.
  • Keep the door blue and the frame navy or cream. Nothing else.

Don't

  • Close the door, flip it, or open it the other way.
  • Add gradients, shadows, outlines or a second accent colour.
  • Set "finexito" in capitals or in another typeface.

Colour

Deep navy, a warm paper and one bright blue. Blue is always the door: the step forward, the primary action. Use it sparingly so it keeps meaning something. The rough mix is shown in the bar below.

Proportion: about 55 paper, 30 navy, 10 slate, 5 blue. Every text pairing passes WCAG AA: navy on paper 16.5:1, cobalt 6.3:1, slate 500 5.2:1, gain 4.7:1, loss 4.7:1, caution 4.7:1, sky on navy 7.0:1, white on cobalt 6.6:1. Gain stays green so it never reads as the brand. Azure and sky are never text on light backgrounds.
